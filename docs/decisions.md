# Decisions and provenance

## DR-001 — Two contracts, one project

Drummer-0 learns a task-dependent signaling system. Drummer Protocol preserves a
defined semantic record exactly. Passing one contract does not imply passing the
other. The practical protocol is an original minimal implementation, not a claim
of full conformance with every earlier proposal.

## DR-002 — Terminal decisions before temporal credit

Enumerating 65 messages computes a terminal expected loss. It does not account
for eight rounds of future effects unless those futures are evaluated. Multi-turn
actor–critic training is explicitly gated and initially uncompleted.

## DR-003 — Capacity, silence, and the revised gate

Five bounded attributes yield 64 identities. A deterministic six-bit code proves
this first task is solvable without a shared candidate order. The optional codec
costs `1 + 6 P(send)` forward bits. The 25% gate requires at least 41.7% omission;
the 40% stretch requires 56.7%. Historical grounding and ACK costs are additional.
The task is intentionally favorable to shared-reference omission; it does not
estimate natural coding conversations' repetition rate.

## DR-004 — Private state is not a covert payload

The proposed eight-dimensional residual remains private. Appraisal is a modeling
variable, not evidence of emotion. SFL labels organize analysis and exact semantic
records; no hand-written SFL grammar constrains the learned alphabet.

## DR-005 — Frozen partners and glosses

Shared weights, independently trained raw cross-play, and aligned cross-play are
reported separately. A gloss trained after freezing receives only the packet and
legitimate audience context. Context-only and packet-shuffle controls are mandatory
before claiming that the gloss decoded a learned symbol.

## DR-006 — Smaller, staged spending

The former $1,000 allocation is superseded by $250. Initial credit funding is $50.
There is no automatic larger-model, new-provider, or multi-GPU escalation. A failed
gate is a reportable outcome, not a reason to keep spending without a new decision.

## DR-007 — Matched warm-up and continuation

The sequential pilot splits the ten-pass ceiling into five compulsory warm-up
passes and at most five continuation passes. All three arms, including the
compulsory comparator, start from the same warm-up checkpoint within a seed;
their optimizer reset and continuation schedule match. This avoids giving only
the optional arm additional training. Shared warm-up compute is booked once.
Early stopping can shorten either phase. Calibration seed 101 is separate from
the five confirmatory seeds.

## DR-008 — Handoff response ontology

The first bootstrap contract called a response field `action` without specifying
whether it meant the work process or requested permission class. Its multistep
strings also relied on undocumented delimiters. Those records are preserved as
instrumentation evidence, not used to score that semantic distinction. The v2
fixture contract distinguishes process from effect and represents ordered steps
structurally. This is a prospective benchmark correction, not a relaxed score.
